About

I'm a licensed air traffic controller at Berbera International Airport in Somaliland, where I've provided aerodrome and procedural control for mixed IFR and VFR traffic since 2022.

I completed ICAO 051 (ATCO basic) in New Zealand and ICAO 052 simulator training in Fujairah, UAE. I speak fluent Arabic and English, and I'm ready for GCAA licence validation, conversion training, and unit endorsement.

Outside the tower, I build software. My main project is an operations system for Hargeisa Tower, now being tested by controllers there, built to replace paper logbooks.
